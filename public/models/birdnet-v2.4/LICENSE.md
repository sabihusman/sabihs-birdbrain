# BirdNET V2.4 model files

The files in this directory (`model.json`, `group1-shard*.bin`, `labels.json`
and everything under `mdata/`) are the official TensorFlow.js export of the
BirdNET GLOBAL 6K V2.4 acoustic model and its V2.4 location/season ("MData")
model, published by the BirdNET team (Stefan Kahl et al., K. Lisa Yang Center
for Conservation Bioacoustics at the Cornell Lab of Ornithology, and Chemnitz
University of Technology).
